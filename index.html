About

Between operations, product, and implementation.

I like solving problems that sit in the awkward middle: too operational to be “just design,” too practical to be “just code.”

01 — BUSINESS-FIRST

My work usually starts with friction: slow reporting, repeated admin, scattered data, manual calculations, unclear handoffs, or a process that only works because one person remembers how it works.

I translate those constraints into simpler systems using the lightest stack that makes sense—spreadsheets, scripts, serverless services, mobile flows, or full digital products.
